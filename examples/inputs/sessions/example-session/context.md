## Background
This is an example oral-history session scaffold. The interviewer is collecting memories, stories, and life details from one or more narrators. The discussion may move between childhood, family members, places lived, school, work, relationships, and anecdotes that come up naturally in conversation.

The system should expect long narrative answers, clarifying questions from the interviewer, short acknowledgments, corrections, and occasional overlap. People may be referred to by relationship terms rather than full names, and uncertain details should be preserved rather than forced into clean attribution.

# Stage Boundaries

Status: current decision

## Boundaries

- Stage 1 owns words and timestamps.
- Stage 2 owns anonymous voices.
- Stage 3 owns speaker identity assignment.
- Stage 4 owns organization for later drafting.

## Rules

- Do not move speaker identity work into Stage 2.
- Do not let Stage 3 rewrite Stage 1 transcript wording.
- Do not let Stage 4 change attribution logic.
- Keep each stage dependent only on inputs and prior-stage outputs.

## Rationale

The stage split exists to keep evidence types separate and to preserve reviewability when speakers discuss the same material.

# Stage 4 Organization Plan

Status: planned

## Goal

Turn the reviewed, speaker-attributed transcript into verbatim source material organized for later narrative drafting.

## Inputs

- Stage 3 identified conversation output
- the original session context as needed for ordering and topic framing

## Output Shape

- one organized artifact per interviewee per session
- one markdown companion per organized artifact

## Non-Negotiables

- preserve verbatim wording
- do not paraphrase
- do not invent chronology
- keep uncertainty and source traceability visible

## Working Direction

- group excerpts by theme
- preserve approximate chronology where it helps review
- keep the organization structure easy to audit against the source transcript
